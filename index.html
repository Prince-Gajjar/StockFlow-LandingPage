Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does StockFlow work without an internet connection? expand_more
StockFlow utilizes an embedded SQLite database engine directly on your Android device. All inventory scans, item counts, and bin shifts are written locally with zero delay. Once connected to Wi-Fi or cellular network, the app automatically syncs with the central server in the background.
What are the minimum Android system requirements? expand_more
StockFlow requires Android 8.0 (Oreo / minSdk 26) or higher. It works seamlessly across standard Android smartphones, rugged industrial handheld terminals (Zebra, Honeywell), and Android tablets.
How do I install the APK file on my Android device? expand_more
Download the APK file directly to your device by clicking the "Download APK" button. When opening the downloaded file, ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is toggled ON in your Android Settings under Security & Privacy.
Can multiple workers use StockFlow simultaneously? expand_more
Yes. Every logged transaction attaches the unique operator ID, timestamp, and device hardware key to prevent item duplication and maintain precise accountability across large warehouse teams.
